About Growth and Active Living Organization of Ontario (GALO)

Learn, Lead, Thrive.

Our Mission

Learn, Lead, Thrive.

To empower youth of all backgrounds and abilities by blending inclusive sports, academic tutoring, and peer mentoring. We build deep community connections across Ontario to raise the next generation of empathetic young leaders.

Our Vision

Belong, dream, achieve.

A vibrant, barrier-free community where every young person has the opportunity to play, learn, and lead with confidence.

V What we stand for
Our values

Connect, Include, Empower, Grow

🤝

Deep Connection

Bringing youth mentors, families, schools, and local organizations together into one strong, supportive community network.

🌈

Universal Inclusion

Creating safe, welcoming spaces where every single participant—regardless of their background or starting point—experiences a profound sense of belonging.

📢

Youth-Led Action

Giving young people real ownership to run front-line programs and drive authentic community impact.

🌱

Holistic Growth

Balancing physical fitness and academic support to nurture the total well-being and personal development of every youth

B Leadership
Executive & staff

The people behind the program

GALO is guided by a volunteer board and a small staff of program coordinators.

President

Adrian Tong

As the President of the Growth and Active Learning organization of Ontario (GALO), I take this role to help lead my team in creating activities and opportunities for our community, especially focusing on our youth and future generations. Through organization programs such as sports, leadership and mentorship programs, I strive to bring people together and encourage teamwork as well as create a positive environment where everyone can stay active, develop new skills and build connections.

Through this role, I focus on supporting my team, guiding our initiative and help turn out ideas into experiences that won't be forgotten. I believe that leadership is about collaboration, dedication and creating opportunities for everyone. By working together, we continue to build a safe, inclusive, and welcoming community where everyone feels valued, encouraged to participate, and inspired to reach their full potential.

Operation & Program Director

Ansley Tong

As the Operations and Program Director at Growth and Active Learning of Ontario (GALO), I oversee event logistics, manage day-to-day operations, and lead the planning and execution of program schedules and formats to ensure the successful delivery of the organization's initiatives. Working closely with the leadership team and volunteers, my focus is on delivering well-organized, high-quality programs that reflect GALO's mission and create meaningful experiences for participants.

Passionate about empowering youth, I am committed to creating opportunities that inspire learning, personal growth, and lasting connections. I strive to ensure our programs are educational, engaging, inclusive, and impactful by helping young people build confidence, develop essential life and leadership skills, and reach their full potential. Through collaboration, thoughtful planning, and a commitment to continuous improvement, I work to create positive experiences that strengthen both the individuals we serve and the broader community.

Volunteer Director

Peiwen Xue

Hello! My name is Peiwen, a member of the Growth and Active Learning organization of Ontario (GALO) where I serve as the Volunteer Director. My role is to recruit volunteers for the different programs and events we host. As the head, my job is not only to recruit volunteers, but also provide support and guidance in their role as volunteers in our facilities so they can succeed as leaders.

Through my actions in GALO, my goal is to create opportunities, and experiences, for youth volunteers to develop valuable skills, gain hands-on experience and grow as leaders. I believe that volunteering is more than a job, it is an opportunity to create self growth and discovery through a positive and inclusive community. I want to strive to foster volunteers who gain qualities of leadership, responsibility, teamwork, and confidence.
